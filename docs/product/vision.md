# Vision

## Elevator Pitch

**STYLIST_AI** is a context-aware shopping and wardrobe companion that:

- Understands *where* you are (environment, role, lifestyle context) and *how you present* (style signals),
- Indexes what you already own into a “digital wardrobe twin,”
- Calibrates your taste via quick, visual preference selection,
- Generates a curated shopping feed with transparent, inspectable reasoning,
- And closes the loop with lifecycle management: wear tracking, condition, return/repair/resale/donate flows.

The experience is designed as a **flow-state interface**: minimal decision fatigue, persistent guidance, and high information density only when requested.

## Product Promise

1. **Shop less, choose better.** Recommendations are grounded in your existing wardrobe and your real contexts.
2. **Always explain “why.”** Every insight and recommendation exposes evidence and deduction steps.
3. **Manage items as long-lived assets.** Ownership and lifecycle actions are first-class, not an afterthought.

## Target Users (Implied by the Mock)

- Professionals and creatives who want outfits tuned to social and work contexts.
- Minimalists and quality-focused shoppers who avoid random purchases.
- Users who already “curate” wardrobes and want the process systematized.
- People who prefer visual interaction over typing.

## Differentiators (What This UX Exploration Emphasizes)

- **Persistent AI assistant**: a bottom “system console” chat bar that follows every screen.
- **Context-first onboarding**: upload environment + outfit cues before shopping.
- **Transparent reasoning UI**: a structured reasoning overlay with sources, steps, and artifacts.
- **Wardrobe as a graph**: shortlist ↔ inventory ↔ capsules ↔ usage ↔ lifecycle actions.
- **Clinical sci‑fi UI language**: sharp, technical, system-like framing with high contrast and calm whitespace.

## What “Success” Looks Like (Spec-Level Metrics)

- **Time-to-first-value**: time from landing to a user seeing a compelling first recommendation feed.
- **Decision confidence**: users report higher confidence because “why” is inspectable.
- **Wardrobe completion**: proportion of user wardrobe indexed over time.
- **Waste reduction proxy**: increased use of repair/resale/donate workflows.
