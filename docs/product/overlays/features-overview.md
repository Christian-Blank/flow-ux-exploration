- Provide a narrative overview of what the system can do.
- Act as a lightweight “capabilities” modal for stakeholders/users.

## Trigger(s)

- Sidebar: “App Capabilities”

## Dismissal

- Close button
- Backdrop click

## Layout

1. Decorative gradient bar header.

   - Title: “System Capabilities”
